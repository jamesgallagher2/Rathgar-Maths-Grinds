About & Why Us

A teacher who knows exactly where students get stuck

We know exactly where students struggle. Our focused approach to learning starts by identifying exactly where gaps in knowledge lie, building a plan around the student, and tracking progress at every stage. We place emphasis on understanding concepts rather that regurgitating memorised content during exams. This means students enter examinations prepared and stress-free.

Every grind is tailored to the students needs. Whether it's shoring up the fundamentals or pushing for a H1, sessions are built around what the student actually needs.

  • Quality tutors with years of mathematical experience
  • Ordinary and Higher Level papers
  • Patient, encouraging teaching style suited to all confidence levels
